Harrogate & Wharfedale U9 Match Rules

Tap Question for Instant Ruling
Defender deliberately heads ball in own penalty box?
IFK ON BOX EDGE No Penalty • No Cards

RULING: NO PENALTY! Do NOT award a penalty or IFK inside the penalty box.

➡️ Award an Indirect Free Kick (IFK) on the nearest parallel penalty box boundary line.

⚠️ Signal by raising one arm straight overhead until touched. Strictly NO yellow or red cards for deliberate heading.

🗣️ Say to coaches/players: "At U9, deliberate heading is an indirect free kick only from the box edge line. There are strictly no cards or penalties for heading."
Ball accidentally strikes player's head?
PLAY ON • NO FOUL

RULING: PLAY ON!

Accidental contact where the ball strikes the head without an intentional heading motion is NOT an offence.

⚠️ If player requires a head injury/concussion check, stop play immediately and restart with a dropped ball to the team that had possession.

Goalkeeper picks up deliberate back-pass?
PLAY ON • LEGAL AT U9

RULING: PLAY ON!

Under-9 goalkeepers ARE permitted to receive deliberate passes from their teammates directly into their hands.

Goalkeeper punts or drop-kicks from hands?
RETAKE • NO PUNTING

RULING: NOT PERMITTED.

Drop-kicks and volleys directly out of the hands are not allowed at U9. Stop play and have them retake using an approved roll, throw, or kick from the ground.

Where do opponents retreat for Goal Kicks & saves?
RETREAT TO HALFWAY

RULING: Halfway Line!

When the goalkeeper gains possession with hands or takes a Goal Kick, all opponents MUST retreat behind the halfway line until the ball is released or kicked into play.

Goalkeeper throws or kicks ball over halfway line?
PLAY ON • ANY DISTANCE

RULING: LEGAL / PLAY ON!

As long as the ball is distributed legally (from the ground or rolled/thrown by hand), it may travel any distance across the halfway line.

Goal scored directly from Kick-In / Touchline?
NO GOAL • GOAL KICK

RULING:

• Pass-In: CANNOT score directly. If kicked directly into opponent's goal untouched, award a Goal Kick.

• Dribble-In: Player takes 2nd touch to enter pitch; shot on goal must be at least the 3rd touch to count!

Can a Goal Kick score directly into opponent's goal?
GOAL COUNTS

RULING: YES, GOAL!

Under FA rules, a goal CAN be scored directly from a Goal Kick into the opposing team's goal.

*(If kicked directly into kicker's own goal, award a corner kick to opponents — no own goal directly).*

Goal scored directly from Kick-Off or Dropped Ball?
NO GOAL • RETAKE

RULING: NO GOAL.

A goal CANNOT be scored directly from a Kick-Off or Dropped Ball. The ball must touch at least one other player before crossing the goal line.

How far must opponents stand back on kicks?
5 YARDS MINIMUM

RULING: 5 Yards (approx. 5 big paces).

Opponents must retreat at least 5 yards on all Kick-Ins, Free Kicks, Corners, and Kick-Offs until the ball is kicked.

Is there offside in Under-9 matches?
STRICTLY NO OFFSIDE

RULING: NO OFFSIDES.

Under-9 football has strictly no offside rule. Players may receive passes in any attacking position.

When and how can rolling substitutions happen?
STOPPAGE + REF PERMISSION

RULING: Up to 3 rolling substitutes per team.

• Substitutions may ONLY take place during a natural stoppage in play.

• Coach must notify and receive the referee's explicit permission before players enter.

• Substituted players may return later in the match.

Are yellow or red cards shown in Under-9?
STRICTLY NO CARDS

RULING: NO CARDS.

Referees do NOT issue yellow or red cards at U9. For persistent fouls or poor conduct, speak calmly to the player and their coach, or request a substitution for a cool-down period.

Spectators behind goal nets or interfering?
HOLD RESTART • CLEAR AREA

RULING: Strictly NO spectators or coaches permitted behind or beside goal nets. Hold restart until area is clear behind the Respect barrier.

🗣️ Referee Touchline Script: "FA rules require all spectators behind the Respect line on the touchline. Nobody is permitted behind the goals. We will restart as soon as that area is clear."

2. Pitchside & Sideline Management
  • Sideline Split: Coaches and substitutes from BOTH teams must stay on one touchline. All spectators must remain on the opposite touchline behind the Respect barrier.
  • Behind the Goals: Strictly NO coaches, substitutes, or spectators are permitted to stand beside or behind the goal nets.
  • Referee Neutrality: Club-provided referees must remain impartial and are strictly prohibited from coaching their own team while refereeing.
🗣️ Verbatim De-escalation Script (Spectators): "FA rules require all spectators behind the Respect line on the touchline. Nobody is permitted behind the goals. We will restart as soon as that area is clear."
3. In-Game Rules & Substitutions
  • Substitutions: Up to 3 rolling substitutes per team. Substitutions may only take place during a stoppage in play with the referee's explicit permission. Replaced players may return later.
  • Offside: There are NO offsides in Under-9 matches.
4. FA Deliberate Heading Rule
  • General Offence: Deliberately heading the ball is not permitted. Accidental contact (ball merely striking the head without intentional motion) is PLAY ON.
  • Standard Restart: Award an Indirect Free Kick (IFK) to the opposing team at the exact location where the deliberate header took place. Signal by raising one arm straight overhead until touched.
  • Defending Penalty Area Exception: If a defender deliberately heads the ball inside their own penalty box, DO NOT award a penalty or an IFK inside the box. Award the IFK on the nearest parallel penalty box boundary line.
  • Sanctions: Do NOT issue yellow or red cards for deliberate heading.
🗣️ Verbatim Script (Heading Dispute): "At U9, deliberate heading is an indirect free kick only. There are strictly no cards or penalties for heading."
5. Goalkeeper Rules & Retreat Line
  • Back-Pass Allowed: Goalkeepers ARE permitted to receive deliberate passes from their own teammates directly into their hands.
  • Retreat Line (Halfway): When the goalkeeper gains possession with their hands or prepares for a goal kick, all opposition players MUST retreat behind the halfway line until the ball is released.
  • Distribution Method: The goalkeeper must release the ball from within their half by rolling, throwing, or kicking it from the ground.
  • No Punting: Drop-kicks and volleys directly out of the hands are NOT permitted. If a goalkeeper punts the ball, stop play and have them retake the restart using an approved ground or hand release.
6. Restarts & Scoring Protocols
  • Touchline Restarts (Kick-In / Dribble-In): Throw-ins are not used. The ball is placed stationary on the touchline; opponents must retreat 5 yards.
  • Pass-In (Touchline): A goal CANNOT be scored directly into the opponent's goal from an initial pass-in. If it goes straight in untouched, award a goal kick to the defending team.
  • Dribble-In (Touchline): The taker may choose to take a second touch to dribble the ball into play. Once dribbling, they are an active player and CAN shoot and score. (The shot on goal needs to be at least the third touch).
  • Goal Kicks: A goal CAN be scored directly into the opposing goal. If a goal kick is accidentally kicked directly into the kicker's own goal, award a corner kick to the opponents (no own goal directly).
  • Kick-Offs & Dropped Balls: A goal CANNOT be scored directly from a kick-off or dropped ball. The ball must touch at least one other player before crossing the goal line.
